Research Interests

I care about the data and training infrastructure this needs as much as about the models.

FLIVR: Motion-Aware Video-to-Video Retrieval with Multi-Vector Representations

Uday Agarwal*, Alik Sarkar*, Yogesh Kumar, Manish Gupta, Anand Mishra
* equal contribution

Under review, 2026  ·  with Microsoft Research India

A two-stage system that treats video itself as the query. Global embeddings shortlist candidates, then a transformer-based late-interaction stage (MeanMaxSim) scores token-level spatio-temporal matches, fused with global similarity via reciprocal rank fusion. 83.0% R@1 on VR-ActivityNet and 53.0% R@1 on SportsMoment, the latter +15.4 over the next-best model. Exhaustive late interaction over ~48M pairwise comparisons runs on a single 48 GB GPU through frame-feature caching and chunked scoring.

An egocentric dataset for procedural understanding and deviation detection

Alik Sarkar, and collaborators at VL2G

Ongoing  ·  supported by the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF)

~120 hours of untrimmed egocentric footage: 10 participants performing 23 tabletop manipulation tasks, each paired with a text and video standard operating procedure. I designed the capture protocol and built the ingestion, annotation and temporal-alignment pipeline end to end. On top of it I work on SOP-conditioned deviation detection — grounding a recording against its reference procedure to localise and classify where an operator departs from it.

Printed OCR for Extremely Low-resource Indic Languages

Alik Sarkar, Ajoy Mondal, Gurpreet Singh Lehal, C. V. Jawahar

CVIP 2024  ·  Oral Presentation

Paper Code

The first large-scale printed-text dataset for five of the nine extremely low-resource official Indian languages, with real and synthetic splits totalling over 1M word images across all nine. We establish CRNN baselines for every language, exceeding 90% word and character recognition rates on most through synthetic pretraining and real-data finetuning.

A multilingual document VLM for 22 Indian languages

A ViT-encoder / LLM-decoder model (GOT-OCR2.0 style) for document understanding across 22 Indian languages and three modalities — printed, handwritten and scene text — replacing a stack of modular ad-hoc systems with one holistic model. I built the synthetic rendering pipeline covering fonts and degradations, and the multi-GPU training setup, at over 1M samples.
